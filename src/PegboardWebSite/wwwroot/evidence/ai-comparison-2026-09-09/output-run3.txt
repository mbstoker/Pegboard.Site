# Badminton club-night software: evidence audit

Test/access date: **9 September 2026**. Club: UK recreational badminton, 18–26 attendees, four courts, mixed strengths, changing availability.

**30 candidates; 29 supplied URLs and one independently located product.** This is a public-documentation evaluation, not an installed-app or live-club trial. ePegboard leads the verified evidence subtotal at **78.5/100**, followed by vsBadminton **61.5** and ServeLeague **61**. **A defensible overall ranking of all 30 cannot yet be calculated.** Eight supplied sites remained unreadable and are U throughout; several other candidates have material unresolved criteria.

The practical recommendation is to trial ePegboard first, alongside vsBadminton. ServeLeague is third by evidence subtotal, with rotating badminton doubles a priority verification. ShuttlePilot is a close alternative worth including if the club favours its round-based workflow. These are provisional trial priorities, not proven performance rankings.

## Method and fixed rubric

The supplied criterion names do not include longer formal definitions. I have retained all 15 names and weights exactly, applying them to the stated club scenario. Compound criteria require evidence for their relevant parts: payment bookkeeping alone earns partial credit; a device listing does not establish resilience; a skill-mode name does not establish a rating system. Live scoring includes recording results while the session is running, not necessarily rally-by-rally input.

**2 = VERIFIED FULL:** operational instructions/rules and relevant limits establish the capability for this club. **1 = VERIFIED PARTIAL:** an explicit vendor claim or only part of the criterion is established. **0 = VERIFIED ABSENT:** explicit evidence establishes non-support. **U = UNVERIFIED:** insufficient evidence, including retrieval failure. Here, “verified” means documentary support was verified; vendor claims were not independently demonstrated.

A feature missing from a page is U, never automatically 0. An unsupported subfeature does not zero a composite criterion when other parts are supported. Thus ePegboard’s payment criterion is 1, although online collection is explicitly absent.

## B. Weighted calculation

For a resolved criterion, contribution = weight × score ÷ 2. Let **V** be the sum of resolved contributions and **M** the sum of weights of U criteria. The requested possible range is **[V, V+M]**, holding existing 0/1/2 judgments fixed. These are evidence-completion bounds, not confidence intervals or ultimate capability bounds: further evidence can also change a current 1.

For all-U candidates, no verified score is reported. Their 0–100 possible range describes complete uncertainty; it does not convert any U to zero.

## E. Material weaknesses of the provisional winner
